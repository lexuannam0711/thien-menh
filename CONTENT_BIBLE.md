# Thần Mệnh — Content Bible

## Campaign

Four chapters move from village and bamboo forest through swamp and mountain regions to Phong Châu. Each chapter owns an objective, area configuration, enemy pool and boss encounter.

## Content counts

Catalog targets: 4 chapters, 5 areas, 3 dungeons, 15 NPCs, 12 enemies, 4 elites, 12 main quests and 16 side quests.

## Tone

Vietnamese-mythology-inspired fantasy. Use respectful fictionalization; avoid presenting invented details as historical fact.
