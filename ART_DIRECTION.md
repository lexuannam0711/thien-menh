# Thần Mệnh — Art Direction

## Target, not current completion

**Premium Vietnamese mythological pixel art**, top-down 2D hack-and-slash, fictional Văn Lang during the eighteenth Hùng king's era. Phaser 4, TypeScript, Vite and existing combat/RPG contracts remain unchanged. Bronze Đông Sơn drum geometry and Chim Lạc silhouettes form the identity, not generic fantasy decoration.

October 12, 2026 source audit: four small generated sprite sheets, procedural scenery, primitive NPCs and three procedural boss bodies. This is a fallback foundation, **not the target direction and not graphics 1.0**. Four upgraded sheets cannot satisfy full-world scope. No browser art approval or device performance measurement is claimed here.

## Art pillars

| Pillar | Production decision | Reject |
|---|---|---|
| Văn Lang identity | Bronze weapons, woven cloth, bamboo, stilt houses, rice, river life, restrained drum/Lạc-bird ornament | Unmotivated imported architecture/heraldry, contemporary cartoon clothing |
| Premium pixel craft | Intentional clusters, coherent density, authored silhouettes/animation, review of generated pixels | Primitive recolors as final creatures; unedited AI concepts as sheets |
| Combat readability | Hero value separation, visible feet, anticipation/contact/recovery; warning before ornament | Glow hiding windup; decorative reach disagreeing with hitbox |
| Mythological restraint | Earth/fire/water divinity and corruption distinguishable by shape and hue | Every boss sharing an outline or only changing palette |
| Small-screen clarity | Evaluate normal display size, spare ornament around text | Unreadable micro-patterns, thin warnings, text beneath touch controls |

Label historical inspiration, legend and original fantasy distinctly in briefs. These are creative rules, not historical-authenticity claims. Do not copy another game's characters, compositions or identity.

## Regional briefs

| Region | Material kit | Atmosphere | Required payoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Làng Lạc Thủy | Stilt houses, earthen path, rice, bamboo fences, smith forge, Bà Mo house, communal tree, landing/well, household props | Warm morning, foliage shadows, cooking smoke, slow water, sparse dust | Lived-in home; services readable, remaining named NPCs integrated |
| Rừng Trúc U Minh | Layered bamboo, moss rocks, roots, ancient tree, grass, corrupted ground | Cool canopy, leaves, bounded fog, violet corruption | Distinct habitats and Hổ arena, not generic green floor |
| Đầm Thiêng | Dark water, lotus, submerged trees, ancient stones, shore transitions | Water mist, simple reflection/ripple, cyan spirit light | Serpent silhouette and safe ground unmistakable |
| Núi Tản Viên | Cliffs, climbing path, waterfall, stone figures, fictional sacred temple | Mountain cloud, restrained sacred gold | Stone boss cracks/slam and terrain depth legible |
| Phong Châu | Văn Lang-inspired capital, court, dwellings, Lạc soldiers, bronze standards | Formal bronze versus Hắc Ấn darkness | Ceremony beyond an arena; final transformations readable |

Dungeon extensions: Động Âm Hồn forest-root/cave; Thủy Cung Phế Tích submerged ruins; Địa Cung Cổ Loa bronze/stone ritual space. Catalog entries are not built maps; playable routes and collision remain dependencies.

## Character/equipment briefs

- Hero: young warrior, strong stance, ancient-inspired cloth, bronze weapon, simple Lạc motif, glowing Thiên Mệnh Ấn; visible feet/body in every region.
- Giáo Đồng: long thrust, narrow reach-aligned streak. Rìu Chiến: broad heavy sweep, weight/recoil. Kiếm Lạc: curved slash rhythm, distinctive blade/trail. Require equipped world visuals, not names or tint alone.
- NPCs: elder oathkeeper, forge worker and Bà Mo differ in pose/tools/costume; all fifteen catalog NPCs need sprites, portraits and role cues before full-content acceptance.
- Bosses: Hổ feline mass/claws/rage; Thuồng Luồng serpent coils/waves; Thạch Tướng stone/cracks; Hắc Ấn faceless void forms/sigils. Each needs authored attacks, phases and death.
